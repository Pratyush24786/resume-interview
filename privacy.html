PRIVACY

Your résumé report stays in your browser.

CareerMatch reads the résumé and job description in your browser to create the report. The standard report does not upload, save, sell, or send the résumé or job description to CareerMatch.

AI interview practice

If you choose to use the AI interview, CareerMatch sends readable résumé text, the job description, and submitted answers to OpenAI to tailor questions and provide feedback. If you record a voice answer, the audio is sent to OpenAI for transcription; the transcript and any submitted code are also sent for coaching. CareerMatch does not save interview sessions. OpenAI processes submitted text under its API terms and data controls; review OpenAI's current privacy information before using this feature.

What else may be collected

If you choose to open a feedback, contact, booking, or payment link, that external service may collect information under its own privacy policy. This website also uses public libraries to read PDF and DOCX files; the files themselves are not sent to those libraries.

Before you use this site

Do not include passwords, financial information, government identity numbers, or other unnecessary sensitive information in a résumé you upload anywhere online. The report and AI interview are educational preparation tools, not hiring guarantees.
